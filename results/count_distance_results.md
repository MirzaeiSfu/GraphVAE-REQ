## MMD bandwidth

The primary raw-count MMD-RBF bandwidth is selected independently for each rule using the median positive pairwise distance among that rule's training counts only, with a fallback of `1.0`. The fixed-bandwidth result uses `σ=1` for every rule. Linear MMD has no bandwidth and is in squared motif-count units, whereas RBF-MMD is dimensionless.
